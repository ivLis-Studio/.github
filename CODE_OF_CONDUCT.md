# Code of conduct

> **한국어 요약**
> ivLyrics의 이슈, PR, Discord에서는 누구나 존중받아야 합니다. 괴롭힘, 차별, 인신공격, 개인정보 공개, 스팸은 허용되지 않습니다. 문제를 보면 me@ivl.is 또는 Discord 운영진에게 알려 주세요.

## Our pledge

We want ivLyrics to be a welcoming place for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, including with people who are new or who write in another language.
- Give and accept constructive feedback. Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Stay on topic, and help keep reports clear and useful.
- Respect people's privacy and the rights of artists and other rights holders.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, and sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information, such as accounts, keys, tokens, or addresses, without permission.
- Spam, advertising, and repeated off-topic posts.
- Sharing pirated content or asking others to help infringe rights.

## Scope

This code applies in ivLis Studio repositories, the ivLyrics Discord server, and other ivLyrics spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems to me@ivl.is or to a Discord moderator. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may edit or remove content, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, depending on how serious and repeated the behavior is.
